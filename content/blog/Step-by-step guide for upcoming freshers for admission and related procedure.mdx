## Fresh Admission Procedure 
<AccordionGroup>
 <Accordion title=”documents required”>
  -Fee paid to KEA  
  -Confirmation Slip 
  -SSLC Marks card or 10th Marks card 
  -Class 12 Marks Card  
  -Study certificate for having studied 7 years in Karnataka. (7 years is minimum)
  -Transfer Certificate tag:
  -migration certificate for CBSE ICSE international or outside Karnataka student only 
  -income and caste certificate ( if applicable)  
  -physically handicapped certificate ( if applicable) 
  -Kannada medium/Rural study (of seat is allotted through this quota) 
  -certificate pertaining to defence/sports (if applicable) 
  -Adhar card 
  - 2 Passport size photos
<Call-out type=”info” title=”Important Reminder”>
-Bring all the original documents along with 2 sets of xerox copies for all the documents mentioned above
-have a PDFs/.jpg of those documents mentioned above under 100kb. 
-Bring pen!!
