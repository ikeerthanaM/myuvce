## Fresh Admission Procedure 
<AccordionGroup>
 <Accordion title=”documents required”>
  <ul>
   <li>Fee paid to KEA</li>  
   <li>Confirmation Slip</li> 
   <li>SSLC Marks card or 10th Marks card</li>
   <li>Class 12 Marks Card</li> 
   <li>Study certificate for having studied 7 years in Karnataka. (7 years is minimum)</li>
   <li>Transfer Certificate</li>
   <li>migration certificate for CBSE ICSE international or outside Karnataka student only</li>
   <li>income and caste certificate ( if applicable)</li>  
   <li>physically handicapped certificate ( if applicable)</li>
   <li>Kannada medium/Rural study (of seat is allotted through this quota)</li>
   <li>certificate pertaining to defence/sports (if applicable)</li> 
   <li>Adhar card</li>
   <li>2 Passport size photos</li>
 </ul>
<Callout type=”info” title=”Important Reminder”>
 <ul>
  <li>Bring all the original documents along with 2 sets of xerox copies for all the documents mentioned above</li>
  <li>have a PDFs/.jpg of those documents mentioned above under 100kb.</li>
  <li>Bring pen!!</li>
 </ul>
</callout>
 </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<ul>
<li>Fill all three forms, take a signature of your parents/guardian, affix your photograph.</li>
<li>Put your original documents and the 2 sets of xerox copies inside the big file given to you</li>
</ul>

### 2. Documents checking
<ul>
 <li>go inside the office when your token number is called</li> 
 <li>they will ask you to give your original documents, xerox copies, all the 3 forms and free receipt.</li>
 <li>after reviewing all they will take all the original documents and keep it to themselves (doubtful).</li>
 <li>The acknowledgement letter will be signed and sealed by them and return it to you, along with the file</li>
</ul>

### 3. Face verification 
<ul>
 <li>take the acknowledgement to the clerks sitting beside the computer. They ask your 12th marks cet numbers, otp so keep your soft copies open in your phone. And take the phone of which number you had given during KCET  registration</li>
 <li>they scan your face. Smile a bit :). After that they give you yet another form titled ‘admission order'  (candidate copy + College copy)</li>
<li>They tag the college copy to the big file. And give you the candidate copy. It is for you, enjoy..</li>
</ul>

### 4. UUMCS registration 
<ul><li>move to the library.. upstairs. Their our IEEE volunteers will be present. Be redy to save random seniors mobile number to send them your soft copies</li> <li>they will ask you to send soft copies to their number, you have to do nothing here. Just answer their questions and give them your admission order. They handle the rest.</li> <li>after the registration, the computer screen shows the unique number called USN or Student registration number. Take it's pic</li> </ul>

### 5. Ledger entry 
<ul><li>go to the section were their is a ledger of your course fill the details, affix photo, sign.</li></ul>
<Callout type=”tip” title=”be ready with soft copies”
 description =”as you have to fill in all the details right from zero like fee receipt number, class 12th marks, roll number”
</Callout>
<ul><li>here you will be given admission number. Which will be filled in the acknowledgement letter </li>

### 6. Return the file
<ul><li>go to office return the file. Go home. Enjoy your holidays</li></ul>

## Change of Branch procedure 
Since your original documents are already with the college. Your process is slightly smoother 
<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title=”Documents Required>
<ul><li>two copies of your new confirmation slip</li> <li>2 copies fee paid to KEA</li> <li>your acknowledgement letter and admission order which was given to you previously</li></ul> 
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 1. Form filling 
<ul><li>again stand in a line. Collect token along with 3 forms which were given during previous round admission</li> <li>fill all three forms go to office collect ‘seat cancellation letter’ fill it and sign</li>
<Callout title=”don't worry by the name ‘seat cancellation”
 description=”this is for cancelling your seat in the previous branch so that you can admitted to new course> 
</Callout>

### 2. Signature of registrar 
<MatricCardGroup>
<MatricCard title=”Defination” 
description=”**register signed set** = <ul><li>old acknowledgement letter</li> <li>old admission order</li> <li>new confirmation slip</li>
/>
</MatricCardGroup>
<ul><li>Take the 4th form you just filled along with ‘registrar signed set’ to the registrar office (minchu block) they will be sitting inside the barricade. Take their signature tell them you are here for branch change</li></ul>

### 3. Inside the office 
 <ul><li>take the ‘registrar signed set' to the office along with the 3 forms you filled+ new confirmation slip.</li> <li>They will check and keep the registrar signed set with themselves. Sign the new acknowledgement letter. </li> <li>And they put new acknowledgement, new confirmation slip and fee receipt in the file</li><ul>

### 4. Face verification 
same as previous 

(From here on the process is not sequential)

### 5. Seat cancellation in UUMCS portal 
Tell the volunteers you are here for branch change and want to cancel the previous branch.

### 6. New UUMCS registration 
Just as you did previously. You have to send soft copies and take the pic of Student registration number.

### 7. Go to the ledger of your previous branch scratch your details(may be volunteers do this satisfying task) and write “branch changed to so and so”

### 8. Return the file back to the office and bye bye
